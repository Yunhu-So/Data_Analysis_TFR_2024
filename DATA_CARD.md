# Data Card (Detailed)

## Entities & Granularity
- National annual aggregates for South Korea (KR).

## Metrics
- tfr, births, cmr, cpi, private_education_expense (proxy).

## Time Coverage
- ~1970s–2020s (varies by series).

## Sources
- Official Korean statistical releases (see original Excel files in `data/raw`).

## Transformations
- Column selection, type casting, reindex by year, basic cleaning.

## Limitations
- Correlational analysis; national averages; uncertainty in simple forecasts.
